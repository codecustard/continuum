Continuum · demo on Sui testnet

One item, two games

A run made in Every One Counts is stored on Walrus and recorded on Sui as a ContentRef. This page is a second game: it reads that object, loads the run, draws it its own way, and can publish a remix whose link back to the original is verified on chain.

Testnet only. Records are untrusted: this page caps their size, escapes their text and only draws what it understands. Spec: content records · Walkthrough: docs/demo.md
